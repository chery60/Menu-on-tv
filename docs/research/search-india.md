# Search research: Indian owners who want a menu on a TV (2026-10-25)

Method: 6 web searches and 1 page fetch. No search-volume tool was used, so there are no volumes here. Anything marked (guess) is my inference, not a sourced fact. Almost every source is a vendor, so numbers are claims, not independent data.

| Query | Who shows up | Pattern |

## What the top pages cover

- [Petpooja, digital menu display guide for Indian QSRs](https://blog.petpooja.com/industry-business-guides/digital-menu-display-guide-indian-restaurants-qsr/): headings are how it works and setup, display types per outlet, what goes on screen, why Indian restaurants switch, where to mount, mistakes before you buy, FAQ. The FAQ asks: works without internet, different menus on different TVs, which TV size, how it differs from a kiosk, staff training.
- [Petpooja, printed vs digital cost and ROI](https://blog.petpooja.com/industry-business-guides/digital-menu-board-vs-printed-menu-cost-roi/): printed menu Rs 3,000 to 8,000 to start and Rs 5,000 to 10,000 per change; Android TV plus mount Rs 14,000 to 22,000; payback claimed within a year. Vendor claim.
- [Petpooja, digital menu glossary](https://blog.petpooja.com/glossary/digital-menu/): QR standee Rs 1,500 to 3,000, display boards Rs 25,000 to 45,000.
- Setup claims on the same site: any Android TV 7.1.1 or newer, 32 inch for counters and 43 inch or larger for walls, about 5 minutes to link, power about Rs 3 to 5 a day for a 43 inch TV, last synced menu keeps playing if Wi-Fi drops. All vendor claims.
- [Scala India guide](https://apac.scala.com/in/resources/blogs/the-ultimate-guide-to-digital-menu-boards-boosting-restaurant-efficiency-in-india): general benefits, urban uptake. Thin on price.
- [IndiaMART seller listing](https://m.indiamart.com/redwolfmedia/digital-display.html): 32 inch cafe display Rs 17,800 to 22,800. Shows buyers also search by hardware and expect a rupee price.
- Global how-to pages: [Juuno](https://juuno.co/usecases/digital-menu-boards), [Yodeck](https://www.yodeck.com/use-cases/restaurant-menu-on-tv/), [AIScreen](https://www.aiscreen.io/digital-menu-board/use-tv-as-menu-board/), [Pickcel](https://www.pickcel.com/blog/a-guide-to-tv-menu-boards/). Steps: smart TV or stick, app, Wi-Fi, upload, schedule dayparts. Cost shown in dollars.
- Software roundups: [Evergreen](https://www.evergreenhq.com/?p=23035), [Crown TV](https://www.crowntv-us.com/blog/best-digital-menu-board-software-in-2026-7-platforms-that-actually-deliver). Features listed: dayparts, POS sync, remote edit, analytics, multi-location.

## Gaps we can own (what they skip)

1. Nobody answers "free" for India with a straight yes or no. Free tiers need a Pi or stick, or are QR menus, not TV menus. We can say plainly what is free.
2. No Indian page shows a worked cost for one café in rupees including what to skip (a box, a kiosk).
3. How-to pages assume a signage app and a login on the TV. "Open a link, no app" is rarely covered (guess: this is our wedge; it matches docs/seo-homepage.md).
4. Price change in minutes, Hindi or regional-language dish names, sold-out switching: only touched lightly by one vendor.
5. No page compares options honestly for a one-outlet owner: printed vs QR vs POS display vs a link on the TV.
6. Fire TV stick and old non-smart TV: no source confirmed it. A clear answer would be unusual in results.

## Ranked page topics (build in this order)

1. **How to show your menu on a TV (no app)**
   - Target: how to display menu on smart TV; how to show menu on TV
   - Intent: how-to, owner has a TV and wants it working today
   - Must answer: what you need (a TV with a browser, or a stick), 4 steps from signup to a menu on the wall, no app install, what if Wi-Fi drops, how to change a price, how long it takes.
2. **Digital menu board price in India**
   - Target: digital menu board price India; digital menu board cost
   - Intent: price and comparison before buying
   - Must answer: rupee cost for one café (TV, stick, software), what we charge and what is free, printed reprint cost to compare, power cost, GST note. Mark vendor figures as ranges, with what we do not know.
3. **Free digital menu board**
   - Target: free digital menu board; free digital menu for restaurant
   - Intent: no budget, wants a free start and the catch
   - Must answer: what is free, for how many screens, any time limit, what needs paying, how this differs from a free QR menu.
4. **Digital menu board for cafés, bakeries and QSR counters**
   - Target: digital menu board for cafe; menu board for cafe
   - Intent: type-specific buyer, wants a fit for their outlet
   - Must answer: layout per outlet type, TV size and mounting, dayparts (breakfast and tea), sold-out, photos, veg marks. One page with sections, not three thin pages.
5. **Digital menu board vs printed menu vs QR menu**
   - Target: digital menu vs printed menu; digital menu board vs QR menu
   - Intent: comparing options
   - Must answer: honest table (cost, change speed, guest experience, setup), when printed or QR is enough, a worked reprint cost.
6. **Which TV for a menu board (smart TV, Android TV, Fire TV stick)**
   - Target: smart TV for restaurant menu; Fire TV stick menu board
   - Intent: hardware check before buying
   - Must answer: size by distance, brightness, which sets and sticks work (verify on our player before publishing; no source confirmed Fire TV), old non-smart TV with an HDMI stick, Wi-Fi vs cable.

Dropped: "best digital menu board software" roundups (global competition, wrong buyer for India-first) and chain or multi-outlet pages (not our wedge yet).
